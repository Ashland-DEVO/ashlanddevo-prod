Ashland Bike Fest

A celebration of ALL bikes and ALL cyclists — film, racing, block parties, live music, food trucks and free social rides.

One weekend, every kind of bike

A full weekend with the NYC-based Bicycle Film Festival, Spring Fling Super D and youth Gromduro races, the family-friendly Ashland AlleyCat, free social rides, block parties, live music, food trucks and more.

Race · Sat 10 AM

Spring Fling Super D & Gromduro

Saturday’s Super D is one long timed stage from top to bottom of parts of Ashland’s trail system, combining trails like Missing Links, Marty’s (No Jump Line or Big Rock Garden), Caterpillar, Lizard and Jabberwocky. The start could be as high as lower Bull Gap, depending on snowmelt, and will be announced at packet pickup.

Sunday is the first race in the Coast to Cascade Gromduro series for riders ages 8–18, featuring Lizard, Jabberwocky and BTI. DEVO hosts the world’s coolest Aid & Cheer Station at the bottom of Lizard — bumpin’ beats, lots of cowbell, great spectating 11AM–1PM.

May 15 · Railroad Park

Ashland Alleycat

A scavenger-hunt style race for community members on bikes of all types. Solve clues, ride the race and collect tokens along the way for a chance to win glory and fabulous prizes. Competitive and non-competitive awards — but we’re all winners when we’re having a good time.

Kitten~5 miles · kids & families
Bobcat~10–15 miles · young adults & adults
  • 10:00 (not sharp) — arrival, registration, get clues
  • 11:00 (sharp!) — race begins
  • 2:00 — lunch & awards ceremony

Suggested donation of $10–20 per rider helps fund the meal and city park use. Bring a helmet — no helmet, no ride — plus water, snacks and sunscreen. Swimwear may be encouraged for an optional challenge!

May 15 · Varsity Theatre · 7–9 PM

Bicycle Film Festival

The NYC-based Bicycle Film Festival — with an audience of one million in 100+ cities worldwide — comes to Ashland. Documentaries, narratives, award-winning directors and emerging talents share equal billing in a curated short film program.

  • A South African all-female cycling group envisions a future beyond their surroundings
  • A film-shot montage of an incredible youth program in Lisbon, Portugal
  • In Mexico City, women, children and non-binary cyclists reclaim the streets
  • Jude’s solo journey through Liberia’s dense rainforest
  • Graham Agassiz, Matt Hunter and Matty Miles on a neon-themed night ride — and more!

BFF Ashland is hosted by Ashland DEVO. All proceeds support DEVO youth mountain biking programs. Bike valet parking by Piccadilly Cycles.
