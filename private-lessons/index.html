Private Lessons

Keep up with your kids now that they’re shredding in their DEVO groups.

Level up at your pace

Book a private lesson with Ashland DEVO coaches, who are certified through the Bike Instructor Certification Program (BICP).

A private lesson pairs up to 2 students with 1 coach. We’ll customize your lesson plan to your needs and skill level, with a comprehensive plan to help you progress. Looking for a larger group? Check the WOMBAT schedule or periodic co-ed intro classes advertised on our social media.

Private lessons intake form

Our coaches are certified through the Bike Instructor Certification Program (BICP) and the Professional Mountain Bike Instructors Association (PMBIA), and head coaches complete NICA Level 1 certification.
