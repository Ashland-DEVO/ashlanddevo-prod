Spring & Fall · Grades K–12

Programs for Kids

Age- and skills-based groups, enough coaches for real attention, and a clear path from first pedal strokes to race season.

Not sure where to start?

Choose your rider’s grade and we’ll point you to the right group.

Pick a grade and we’ll point you to the right group.

Sessions are held Spring and Fall, 1–2 hours depending on age and group. Head coaches complete DEVO coach clinics, background checks, NICA Level 1 certification and first aid/CPR, with parents and experienced peers as volunteers and mentors.

Kindergarten

Bear Cubs

Solid basics of their bike and the systems involved.

Details

Bear Cubs get comfortable with a basic understanding of their bikes, including complete mastery of braking, starting and stopping pedaling. Cubs may or may not ride on trails; the goal is to build the foundation and a love of riding, preparing riders to advance to the Coyotes.

1st grade

Coyotes

Growing strength to add new skills.

Details

As riders grow, they develop the strength to incorporate new skills, including climbing, pumping and bike/body separation, building them progressively with more trail riding days.

2nd & 3rd grades

Cougars

Two options, both focused on FUN.

Details

Skills Group — for riders new to DEVO: standing while riding, pumping, cornering, trail scanning, braking, trail etiquette and climbing/shifting. Some riders benefit from multiple Skills sessions.

Trails Group — requires one semester of Skills and a coach recommendation. Riders confidently ride singletrack (level pedals, braking with both brakes, riding BTI and Jabberwocky without walking). Longer rides, more climbing, plus jumps, drops and roll-downs.

Please sign up for the class that describes your rider’s current skill level. Not sure? Contact us.

4th & 5th grades

Jr. Vultures

Skills Group or Trails Group.

Details

The Skills Group is for riders new to DEVO; the Trails Group focuses a bit more on trail riding and requires a semester of Skills plus a coach recommendation. Both are focused on fun.

All 5th grade riders are invited to participate in the Rogue Composite Vultures as they enter 6th grade — as a team rider, or through the Fall Ospreys program for exposure without the team commitment.

Advanced 3rd–5th

Rattlesnakes

Progressive skills for riders ahead of the curve.

Details

A semester of DEVO, available program space, and a coach recommendation are required. Rattlesnakes are comfortable on all blue trails in the Ashland Watershed and many blacks, and focus on front/rear wheel lifts, jumping, drops, cornering, pumping and controlling a slide. They’re encouraged to try the DEVO Downhill Series and often graduate to Gravity, XC or Vultures.

Middle & high school

Ospreys

The on-ramp for new 6th–8th graders.

Details

For all 6th–8th graders new to mountain biking. Ospreys spend lots of time on trails and dirt roads practicing skills, and start and finish at the same locations as the Vultures with their own coaches and peers. Ospreys is a prerequisite for Middle School Gravity and XC.

Middle & high school

DEVO XC

Improve cross-country riding.

Details

Requires at least one semester of Ospreys or Rogue Composite Vultures. Riders learn to prepare for longer rides — nutrition and hydration, self-sufficiency and pacing. A growth mindset and dedication to physically challenging riding is required.

Middle & high school

DEVO Gravity

10 weeks of enduro & downhill skills.

Details

Requires at least one semester of Ospreys or Vultures. Cornering, pumping, jumps/drops, technical terrain and racing techniques in the Ashland Watershed, Mountain of the Rogue and Forest Park, with at least two coach-hosted airbag sessions. Classes coincide with DEVO Downhill races and culminate at the annual Shasta Youth Enduro.

NICA team · 6th–12th

Rogue Composite Vultures

Our NICA race team, competing in the OICL.

Details

The Rogue Composite Vultures team is for riders in 6th–12th grades and competes in the Oregon Interscholastic Cycling League (OICL). Coached by DEVO and part of the Ashland High School Mountain Bike Club.

Spring series

DEVO Downhill Series

An annual series of spring races.

Details

Races are typically on Wednesdays at varying locations, with the goals of building community, teaching, introducing riders to downhill/enduro racing and having fun.

6th–12th · Winter

DEVO Fitness

Stay connected with your bike buds all winter.

Details

Learn the basics of proper weight training and injury prevention from local CrossFit coaches, with gravel rides when weather allows. Classes are at Iron Haven Fitness in Ashland; open to any 6th–12th grade Rogue Composite Vulture or DEVO Gravity rider.

Community

DEVO Community

Volunteers, parents and riders growing together.

Details

DEVO runs on parents, volunteers and experienced peer riders. Interested in coaching or helping out? Get in touch. Women and young adult girls can also join WOMBAT.

Qualified, trained coaches

Certified by the industry’s leading instructor programs

DEVO coaches are certified through the Bike Instructor Certification Program (BICP) and the Professional Mountain Bike Instructors Association (PMBIA), and DEVO head coaches complete NICA (National Interscholastic Cycling Association) Level 1 coach certification — the professional standards for teaching mountain biking safely and well.

  • Required DEVO coach clinics and background checks
  • NICA Level 1 certification and first aid/CPR before leading groups
  • Ongoing training funded by your donations (BICP Level 1, Wilderness First Aid)
