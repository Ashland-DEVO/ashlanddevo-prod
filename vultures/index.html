Rogue Composite Vultures

Ashland DEVO’s cross-country race team — beginners welcome, nobody sits on the bench, and everybody gets to race.

Team racing, family friendly

Caw caw! Meet the Vultures

The Rogue Composite Vultures are a cross-country mountain bike team for riders in grades 6–12 from across the Rogue Valley, racing in the Oregon Interscholastic Cycling League (OICL), a regional league of NICA, the National Interscholastic Cycling Association.

The team is built and coached by Ashland DEVO and operates within the Ashland High School Mountain Bike Club, one of the fall club sports listed by Ashland High School Athletics. “Composite” means riders from different schools and communities ride together as one team.

Everyone participates, there are no cuts, and nobody rides the bench. Beginners are welcome — you don’t need racing experience to join.

What race day feels like

Competitive only as much as you want

Races are family-friendly outings in beautiful places, with free onsite camping for families who enjoy it. It’s racing, but it is only as competitive as you want it to be — and the atmosphere is magical. The Vultures cheer for their teammates and for every rider on the course.

  • Team tent with warming fires, food, water and bike maintenance support
  • Warm-ups and stretching together, cheering each other on
  • Personal goals for every rider — performance, adventure, attendance, or best of all, process-based
  • Courses are loops of about 5 km and 500 ft of climbing per lap, mostly on green trails
  • Riders race their own grade: 6th graders do 1–2 laps, the fastest high schoolers do 4
Our league

NICA & the Oregon League

NICA — the National Interscholastic Cycling Association — runs youth mountain bike leagues across the country, with a mission of helping young people become the best version of themselves, both on and off the bike.

The Oregon Interscholastic Cycling League (OICL) is NICA’s Oregon league, founded in 2017 with its first race season in 2018. It produces races, clinics and camps for middle and high school students and trains the adults who coach local teams. The Vultures race in the league’s south conference alongside teams from Klamath Falls, South Coast, Umpqua Valley, Eugene, Corvallis, Bend and Prineville.

DEVO head coaches complete NICA Level 1 coach certification, along with DEVO coach training, background checks and first aid/CPR.

Season & costs

What a season looks like

Preview note: these are placeholder details for the 2027 season, based on 2025 figures. Please confirm current dates and fees when you register.

Typical timeline

  1. Spring — registration opens

    Registration typically opens in early May. A scholarship application is available for families who need support.

  2. June & August — practices

    Practices start in June, take a July break, then resume in August through mid-October. Most are in Ashland, with occasional rides at Mountain of the Rogue, Forest Park and Prescott Park.

  3. Late Aug – mid Oct — race weekends

    Five NICA weekends in places such as Prineville, Mt. Bachelor, Klamath Falls, Newport and Salem. Riders must ride 3 races to qualify for the final state championship.

  4. October — wrap-up

    An end-of-season party and ride celebrates the team.

Common questions

Do I have to race?

No. Practice-only membership is available. Racing is optional, and families can sign up for NICA race weekends separately.

My rider is brand new to mountain biking.

Welcome! The Vultures are our beginner-friendly cross-country group. Younger or newer riders can start with the 6th–8th grade Ospreys program, which practices alongside the team without the full commitment.

My rider prefers downhill and jumps.

Take a look at DEVO Gravity — a 10-week program for enduro and downhill skills, with races in the spring.

Where can I see how the team is doing?

Read our 2025 race reports and the archive of past seasons.

Who do I contact?

Contact us and we’ll connect you with the Team Director. Ashland High School Athletics also lists the club under Club Sports → Mountain Bike.

Qualified, trained coaches

Certified by the industry’s leading instructor programs

DEVO coaches are certified through the Bike Instructor Certification Program (BICP) and the Professional Mountain Bike Instructors Association (PMBIA), and DEVO head coaches complete NICA (National Interscholastic Cycling Association) Level 1 coach certification — the professional standards for teaching mountain biking safely and well.

  • Required DEVO coach clinics and background checks
  • NICA Level 1 certification and first aid/CPR before leading groups
  • Ongoing training funded by your donations (BICP Level 1, Wilderness First Aid)
