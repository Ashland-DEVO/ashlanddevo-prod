Women on Mountain Bikes and Trails

WOMBAT

Growing the sisterhood — a fun, safe place for women and young adult (YA) girls to ride bikes.

Clinics for women & YA girls

Ride with the sisterhood

Ashland DEVO is stoked to continue its newest program, offering beginner and intermediate skills and trail riding clinics for women and young adult girls.

Browse the clinics below, then register on LeagueApps. Watch our website and social media for new dates.

Clinic descriptions

Choose your level

All clinics emphasize confidence building and “challenge by choice.” Class max: 8 riders.

2 hours · Ages 13+

Beginner 1 — MTB Skills

For women and YA girls (13+) new to mountain biking, coached by certified women instructors including a BICP 1 coach. Go over the 3 essentials and practice the basics to prepare for fun on the trails.

3 hours

Beginner 1 — Trail Riding

For women new to mountain biking ready to get on trails. Easy singletrack and the fundamental skills needed to be safe and have fun, taught by a BICP-certified woman instructor.

3 hours

Beginner 2 — Trail Riding

An introduction to riding beginner trails. This group will encounter common technical features and may session to practice challenges. Taught by a BICP-certified woman instructor.

3 hours · up to 1,500 ft

Intermediate 1 — Trail Riding

Foundational skills and ability to ride lower Ashland watershed trails like Lizard, BTI and Jabberwocky. Longer rides with more elevation gain than Beginner 1 and 2 (up to 1,500 feet of climbing).

Our coaches are certified through the Bike Instructor Certification Program (BICP) and the Professional Mountain Bike Instructors Association (PMBIA), and head coaches complete NICA Level 1 certification.

Prefer one-on-one coaching? See Private Lessons.

Inspiring wellness in kids by building grit, resiliency, and camaraderie through mountain biking.
