Our philosophy

The prescription is simple: go outside.

Today’s kids face screen time, unhealthy foods and a world of instant gratification. Mountain biking pushes back — it’s challenging, takes effort and requires determination.

DEVO programs help kids overcome obstacles and fears, building self-confidence and what we love to call grit, with teamwork, peer support and a shared goal: have fun, ride safely, respect our environment and grow our skills.

Race team · Grades 6–12

Rogue Composite Vultures

Our NICA cross-country team races in the Oregon Interscholastic Cycling League as part of the Ashland High School Mountain Bike Club. Beginners welcome, no cuts, and anyone can race.

Qualified, trained coaches

Certified by the industry’s leading instructor programs

DEVO coaches are certified through the Bike Instructor Certification Program (BICP) and the Professional Mountain Bike Instructors Association (PMBIA), and DEVO head coaches complete NICA (National Interscholastic Cycling Association) Level 1 coach certification — the professional standards for teaching mountain biking safely and well.

  • Required DEVO coach clinics and background checks
  • NICA Level 1 certification and first aid/CPR before leading groups
  • Ongoing training funded by your donations (BICP Level 1, Wilderness First Aid)
