Our story

About Ashland DEVO

Born in 2017 from a clear local need for more youth-oriented mountain biking — and built on grit, resiliency and camaraderie.

Mission

Wellness through mountain biking

Ashland DEVO inspires wellness in our youth by building grit, resiliency and camaraderie through mountain biking.

DEVO means development. Ashland DEVO was born in 2017, rising from the clear need for more local youth-oriented opportunities in mountain biking.

Philosophy

The prescription is simple:
go outside.

Today’s kids are facing serious threats to their health and wellbeing due to unhealthy foods, excessive screen time, and a world of instant gratification. How can our children thrive?

With millions of acres of public lands available to everyone, the opportunities are immense — and the health benefits are backed by science.

So, why mountain biking?

Riding bikes in the mountains is challenging, takes effort and requires determination. DEVO programs encourage kids to overcome obstacles and fears, building self-confidence and what we love to call “grit.” We might be biased, but we believe mountain biking builds resilience that kids carry into the wider world — a stronger sense of self-assuredness, self-reliance and ownership of the places they ride.

Process

How riders progress

A series of age- and skills-based courses in a weekly format. Sessions run Spring and Fall; classes last 1–2 hours depending on age and group ability.

  1. Learn the fundamentals

    Riders move through classes learning the basics of mountain biking and growing their skills, in age- and ability-specific groups that encourage peer learning and support.

  2. Ride new places

    Once they have the dexterity and confidence for the next step, riders explore new trails that hone what they’ve learned and introduce new skills.

  3. Coached by trained leaders

    Head coaches complete DEVO coach clinics, background checks, NICA Level 1 certification and first aid/CPR before leading groups. Parents and experienced peer riders serve as volunteers and mentors.

  4. Level up

    As the program grows, so do niche options — Gravity, XC, the Rogue Composite Vultures race team and more — for riders ready to take their skills further.

Vision

A generation of riders built on common goals

Having fun, riding safely, respecting our environment, and growing our bike skills. DEVO encourages teamwork, builds peer support and promotes growing together through challenges.

This is our community, our goal, and our vision. This is Ashland DEVO. Come join us!
